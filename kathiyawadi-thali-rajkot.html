Our Kathiyawadi Specialties

From the dry-roasted stone-ground spices to pure cow ghee sourced from Gondal, we bring the home-style tastes of rural Saurashtra to University Road.

  • Ringan No Olo: Roasted eggplant cooked with spring onions and spices.
  • Sev Tameta Nu Shaak: Classic sweet and spicy tomato curry.
  • Bajra Rotlo & Butter Bhakhri: Thick, earthy breads cooked on clay griddles.
  • Dahi Tikhari: Warm tempered spiced local yogurt.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Jalaram's Kathiyawadi food authentic?

We strictly avoid commercial masala mixes. All our spices are stone-ground fresh daily. Furthermore, we cook exclusively in pure A2 Gir cow ghee.

Can I order specific Kathiyawadi dishes without onion and garlic?

Absolutely. We offer Jain variations for almost all our Kathiyawadi dishes, including Sev Tameta and Kofta Curries. Please inform your waiter beforehand.

Are you open for dinner?

Yes, Jalaram Restaurant is open daily from 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM on University Road, Jalaram Nagar, Rajkot.
