03Our Story

Bringing the heart of
Kathiyawad to Rajkot.

Launched in December 2025, Jalaram Restaurant was founded by Mayurbhai to share the authentic, rustic flavours of Saurashtra. Having worked in the culinary domain for years, Mayurbhai brings rich restaurant experience and deep-rooted passion to every single dish.

We specialize in traditional Kathiyawadi dishes like our signature Ringan No Olo, Sev Tameta Nu Shaak, and bajra rotla, alongside classic Gujarati, Punjabi, and street-style fast food. Every recipe is prepared with hand-pounded spices, pure cow ghee, and fresh vegetables sourced from the morning mandi.

Though we are a young restaurant, our dedication to quality, hygiene, and authentic tastes has quickly made us a favorite dining destination in Rajkot. We invite you to experience the warmth of home-style Kathiyawadi hospitality.

05Why Jalaram

Beyond taste —
a way of cooking.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries four promises — the same four we made on day one, in 1995.

01

Hand-Pounded Spices

No commercial masala. Every spice is dry-roasted and stone-ground fresh each morning in our kitchen.

02

100% Pure Veg

A strictly vegetarian kitchen. We also offer Jain variants — no onion, no garlic, no root veg — on every dish.

03

A2 Cow Ghee Only

We cook only in pure A2 Gir cow ghee — sourced from a single farm near Gondal. No vanaspati, ever.

04

Mandi-Fresh Daily

Vegetables arrive at 6 AM from Rajkot mandi. We close when we sell out — no yesterday's food, ever.
