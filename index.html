03Our Story

A kitchen that began
with a single rotla.

In 1995, in a small lane in Rajkot, Mr. Prabhudas Hirani set up a single-burner stove and a wooden cart. He served one dish — rotla with ringan no olo — to factory workers heading home after long shifts.

Three decades later, that cart has grown into Jalaram Restaurant — a 120-seat dining destination loved across Saurashtra. But the recipe hasn't changed: hand-pounded spices, pure ghee, fresh vegetables from the morning mandi, and the same warmth Mr. Hirani served with on day one.

Today, three generations of the Hirani family run Jalaram — preserving the soul of Kathiyawad while welcoming the flavours of Gujarat, Punjab and the streets of Mumbai into our kitchen.

05Why Jalaram

Beyond taste —
a way of cooking.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries four promises — the same four we made on day one, in 1995.

01

Hand-Pounded Spices

No commercial masala. Every spice is dry-roasted and stone-ground fresh each morning in our kitchen.

02

100% Pure Veg

A strictly vegetarian kitchen. We also offer Jain variants — no onion, no garlic, no root veg — on every dish.

03

A2 Cow Ghee Only

We cook only in pure A2 Gir cow ghee — sourced from a single farm near Gondal. No vanaspati, ever.

04

Mandi-Fresh Daily

Vegetables arrive at 6 AM from Rajkot mandi. We close when we sell out — no yesterday's food, ever.
